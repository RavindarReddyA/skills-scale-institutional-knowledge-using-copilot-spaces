# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers provide technical delivery leadership, support team health, and ensure engineering capacity and execution align with project and product commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Manage engineering capacity, staffing, and delivery health
- Support technical planning, estimation, and prioritization
- Identify and coordinate cross-team technical dependencies
- Coach developers and remove execution barriers
- Partner with developers on technical risks, quality, and maintainability

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to align capacity, schedules, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Work with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into feasible engineering plans
- Support Developers through planning, design reviews, and escalation of technical blockers
- Provide technical delivery updates to stakeholders and help inform trade-off decisions

---

## Security and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads identify security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements and help teams address them throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements, designs, and implementations for security and compliance risks
- Define or validate security controls and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate security testing, assessments, and remediation plans
- Track security and compliance risks in the risk register
- Advise teams on incident readiness and required escalation paths

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to incorporate security, privacy, and compliance needs into product requirements
- Work with Developers and Engineering Managers on secure design, implementation, testing, and remediation
- Work with Project Managers to track risks, dependencies, decisions, and required approvals
- Provide risk-based guidance to stakeholders before release and escalate material concerns when necessary

---

## QA and Release Managers

### Role Summary
QA and Release Managers coordinate validation and release readiness so that features meet acceptance criteria and can be deployed safely and reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Define and coordinate test strategy, test coverage, and quality gates
- Track acceptance criteria, defects, regression results, and release blockers
- Coordinate staging validation, smoke tests, and production verification
- Maintain release readiness checklists and rollback or mitigation plans
- Communicate release status, known issues, and go/no-go recommendations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and stakeholders to confirm acceptance criteria and feature readiness
- Work with Developers on testability, defect resolution, automated tests, and release fixes
- Work with Engineering Managers to assess technical quality, capacity, and release risk
- Work with Project Managers to maintain milestones, dependencies, status reports, and escalation paths

---

## Customer Success and Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons represent customer-facing teams in project planning and execution, ensuring that user feedback, operational concerns, and support readiness inform delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback, support trends, and recurring issues
- Identify customer impact, adoption risks, and support needs during planning
- Coordinate documentation, enablement, and support readiness for releases
- Share post-release feedback and incident information with the delivery team
- Help prioritize improvements based on customer and operational evidence

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to translate customer needs and feedback into backlog priorities and success measures
- Work with Project Managers on stakeholder updates, release communications, and escalation of customer-impacting risks
- Work with QA and Release Managers to prepare support teams and validate customer-facing behavior
- Work with Developers and Engineering Managers to provide reproducible issue details and inform remediation priorities

---

## Data and Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners define how project outcomes will be measured and provide the instrumentation, reporting, and analysis needed for evidence-based decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans, key performance indicators, and baselines
- Identify required instrumentation, data sources, and dashboard updates
- Validate data quality and report on product and delivery outcomes
- Analyze adoption, usage, performance, and success metrics after release
- Recommend improvements based on observed results and trends

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define measurable success criteria and evaluate customer and business outcomes
- Work with Developers and Engineering Managers to plan instrumentation, telemetry, and data quality improvements
- Work with Project Managers to include measurement activities and reporting milestones in the project plan
- Work with stakeholders to communicate results and support data-informed decisions about follow-up work

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Use the interaction guidance to clarify ownership, handoffs, decision-making, and escalation paths across the project lifecycle.
